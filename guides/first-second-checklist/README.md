# The first-second checklist

Most people decide in about a second whether to keep watching, often with the sound off. Instagram measures it directly: the share of viewers who leave in the first three seconds (your skip rate) feeds how far a reel travels. Run every reel through this list before you post.

| # | Check | Why |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | **Text on screen by half a second** | Muted viewers decide from the text. If it isn't readable by 0.5 s, it doesn't exist. |
| 2 | **Two lines, max** | One idea, big enough to read on a phone (about 4% of the frame's height or more). |
| 3 | **Frame one shows the result, not the setup** | No empty card, blank screen or "input waiting to be typed". Show what they get. |
| 4 | **Say who it's for** | Name the viewer ("if you post reels…") so the right people stay and the wrong ones leave early. |
| 5 | **Put a real number in it** | Specific reads as true. Use a real number, name or timeframe. |
| 6 | **Open a question you don't answer yet** | Give them a reason to stay past second five. Answer it later, don't drag it to the end. |
| 7 | **Ask for the comment last, never first** | "Comment X" goes after the hook, never as the hook. |
| 8 | **Cut every pause** | Dead air at the start is a skip. Keep breathers only after a line that has to land. |
| 9 | **Change the picture every few seconds** | Something new on screen every 2–5 seconds: a cut, a card, a real action. |
| 10 | **Caption every line for sound off** | Every spoken line on screen, and key claims as text too. |
| 11 | **Check your 3-second skip rate, not your views** | Views tell you what happened. Skip rate tells you why. |
