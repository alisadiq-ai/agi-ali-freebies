# Homework codes: 10 codes for helping your child with homework

Type a code, then paste the question or worksheet. It's one free Claude skill: [homework-codes](../../skills/homework-codes/SKILL.md).

The first time, Claude asks your child's age or school year and the subject. It helps your child learn; it doesn't do the homework. Final answers go only to you.

| # | Code | What Claude does |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | Hint ladder | One hint at a time, each a small step closer, waiting for your child's try in between. Never the final answer |
| 2 | Parent refresher | For you: how the topic is taught now, in about a minute, with one worked example |
| 3 | Check the work | Marks each answer right or not yet, and points to the step that went off |
| 4 | Practice set | Five more like it, easiest first, with the answer key in a separate block for you |
| 5 | Story problem | The same problem, starring something your child loves |
| 6 | Their level | Rewrites the instructions in words a child that age understands |
| 7 | Game mode | A quick kitchen-table game: a points quiz, a timer race or a guess-the-word round |
| 8 | Spelling quiz | This week's words, one at a time, with a tip for every miss |
| 9 | Reading check | Five questions about what they read, and what a good answer would include |
| 10 | Tiny steps | One big task becomes three small ones, plus one calm line for you to say |

**Good to know:** use it alongside your child, not instead of you or their teacher. If the school uses a particular method, tell Claude and it will follow it.

## Install

- **Claude app:** download the [homework-codes](../../skills/homework-codes) folder, zip it, and upload the zip in Customize > Skills (works on every plan).
