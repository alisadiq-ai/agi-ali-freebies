# Work codes: 10 codes that turn Claude into your chief of staff

Type a code, then paste your emails, notes or to-do list. It's one free Claude skill: [work-codes](../../skills/work-codes/SKILL.md).

| # | Code | What Claude does |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | Inbox triage | Sorts your emails into reply now, hand off, schedule or ignore, and drafts the replies |
| 2 | Meeting to actions | Decisions, then who does what by when, plus a three-line recap to send |
| 3 | BLUF | Bottom line up front: your ask goes in the first line |
| 4 | Decision memo | One page: two or three options, the trade-offs, one recommendation |
| 5 | Say no | A short, polite no that keeps the relationship, in a firmer and a softer version |
| 6 | Prioritise my week | Your list goes in; the three things that matter come out, with what to delegate or drop |
| 7 | One-pager | A plan or a long document on one page: goal, problem, plan, cost, risks, ask |
| 8 | Delegate | A brief someone else can run without coming back with questions |
| 9 | Pre-read | The five points that matter in a long document, and three questions to ask in the meeting |
| 10 | Status update | Rough notes become a five-line update: done, next, blocked, what you need |

**Good to know:** everything is a draft. You read it, change it and send it yourself; Claude never sends anything. It won't invent numbers, names or dates, and asks when something is missing.

## Install

- **Claude app:** download the [work-codes](../../skills/work-codes) folder, zip it, and upload the zip in Customize > Skills (works on every plan).
- **Claude Code:** `npx skills add alisadiq-ai/agi-ali-freebies`, or copy the folder into `~/.claude/skills/`.
