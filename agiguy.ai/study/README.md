# Exam mode: 10 codes for the night before

Type a code before your topic, or paste your material after it. It's one free Claude skill: [exam-mode](../../skills/exam-mode/SKILL.md).

| # | Code | What Claude does |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | Past paper | Paste past papers; it ranks the topics that keep coming up |
| 2 | Cheat sheet | Everything likely to be tested, on one page |
| 3 | Exam sim | A timed mock exam in your exam's style, then it marks it |
| 4 | Explain my mistake | Paste your wrong answer; it finds the exact step that went wrong |
| 5 | Socratic | Only questions, one at a time, until you get it yourself |
| 6 | Memory palace | Turns a list you can't remember into a walk you can't forget |
| 7 | Spaced plan | A day-by-day review plan up to your exam date |
| 8 | Rubric | Grades your answer against the mark scheme, line by line |
| 9 | Worked example | Solves one step by step, then gives you a twin to try |
| 10 | Connect | Links the topic to something you already know |

## Install

- **Claude Code:** `npx skills add alisadiq-ai/agi-ali-freebies`, or copy the [exam-mode](../../skills/exam-mode) folder into `~/.claude/skills/`.
- **Claude app:** download the exam-mode folder, zip it, and upload the zip in Claude's Skills settings.

Then type, for example, `Exam sim: cell biology` or `Explain my mistake:` and paste your working.

Learning a topic from scratch? The first ten codes (ELI5, 80/20, Feynman and more) are in [learn-anything](../../skills/learn-anything/SKILL.md).
