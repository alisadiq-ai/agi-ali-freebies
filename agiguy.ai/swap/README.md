# 9 more paid apps with a free twin

Nine more subscriptions, each with a free app that does the job. Together that's about **$133 a month**, at the lowest price each paid app lists on its own pricing page (USD, checked November 20, 2026; most of those are annual plans, so month-to-month costs more).

| # | Instead of | Use | What you get | Get it | Good to know |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | ChatGPT Plus ($20/mo) | **Jan** | A ChatGPT-style app that runs AI on your own computer, privately | [jan.ai](https://jan.ai) · [janhq/jan](https://github.com/janhq/jan) | Apache-2.0. Local models are weaker than the best paid ones and need a decent amount of RAM |
| 2 | Notion ($10/mo) | **AFFiNE** | Docs, wiki and whiteboard in one app that works offline | [affine.pro](https://affine.pro) · [toeverything/AFFiNE](https://github.com/toeverything/AFFiNE) | Open source (MIT for most of the code). The free plan has no time limit |
| 3 | Zoom ($14.16/mo) | **Jitsi Meet** | Video calls in your browser, no account needed | [meet.jit.si](https://meet.jit.si) · [jitsi/jitsi-meet](https://github.com/jitsi/jitsi-meet) | Apache-2.0 |
| 4 | Loom ($15/mo) | **Cap** | Record your screen and camera, share a link in seconds | [cap.so](https://cap.so) · [CapSoftware/Cap](https://github.com/CapSoftware/Cap) | AGPL-3.0. The free plan is for personal use |
| 5 | Todoist ($5/mo) | **Super Productivity** | To-do list, time tracker and daily planner, offline and private | [super-productivity/super-productivity](https://github.com/super-productivity/super-productivity) | MIT |
| 6 | Adobe Acrobat ($14.99/mo) | **Stirling-PDF** | Merge, split, sign, compress and OCR PDFs on your own computer | [Stirling-Tools/Stirling-PDF](https://github.com/Stirling-Tools/Stirling-PDF) | Open core (MIT core). Free for personal use |
| 7 | Adobe Lightroom ($9.99/mo) | **darktable** | Edit RAW photos and batch-fix colour | [darktable.org](https://www.darktable.org) · [darktable-org/darktable](https://github.com/darktable-org/darktable) | GPL-3.0 |
| 8 | Adobe Premiere Pro ($21.99/mo) | **Kdenlive** | A full multi-track video editor | [kdenlive.org](https://kdenlive.org) | GPL-3.0 |
| 9 | Adobe Photoshop ($21.99/mo) | **GIMP** | Edit, retouch and cut out photos | [gimp.org](https://www.gimp.org) | GPL-3.0. For drawing and painting, try [Krita](https://krita.org) |

Prices: Adobe's pages list annual plans prepaid (Photoshop and Premiere Pro US$263.88/yr, Lightroom US$119.88/yr, Acrobat Standard US$14.99/mo on an annual plan); Notion Plus $10/mo yearly; Zoom Pro $14.16/mo yearly; Loom Business $15/mo yearly; Todoist Pro $5/mo yearly; ChatGPT Plus $20/mo per OpenAI's help centre.
