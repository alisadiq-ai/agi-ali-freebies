# 9 paid apps with free, open-source twins

Together these nine cost $151.96 a month at their cheapest paid plans. Each one has a free, open-source app that does the same job.

Prices are in USD, billed monthly, checked on November 9, 2026. They change, so check each pricing page before you cancel.

| Paid app | Cheapest paid plan | Free twin | What the twin does | Good to know |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Figma | $20/month (Professional, full seat) | [Penpot](https://github.com/penpot/penpot) | Design and prototyping in your browser | Free cloud plan, or host it yourself |
| Midjourney | $10/month (Basic) | [ComfyUI](https://github.com/Comfy-Org/ComfyUI) | Makes AI images and video on your own computer | Needs a decent computer (from 4 GB of VRAM and 8 GB of RAM). It runs open models, not Midjourney's, and each model has its own licence |
| Wispr Flow | $15/month (Pro) | [Handy](https://github.com/cjpais/Handy) | Press a key, speak, and it types into any app, offline | Plain transcription, with no AI rewriting. Mac, Windows and Linux |
| Zapier | $29.99/month (Professional) | [Activepieces](https://github.com/activepieces/activepieces) | No-code automations and AI agents | Free if you host it yourself, or a free cloud plan with 1,000 credits a month. Fewer integrations than Zapier |
| Otter.ai | $16.99/month (Pro) | [Meetily](https://github.com/Zackriya-Solutions/meetily) | Records, transcribes and summarises meetings on your own machine | Windows and Apple-Silicon Macs. Telling speakers apart is in its paid tier |
| DocuSign | $15/month (Personal) | [DocuSeal](https://github.com/docusealco/docuseal) | PDF forms and e-signatures | Unlimited if you host it yourself. The free cloud plan sends 10 signature requests a month |
| Microsoft 365 | $9.99/month (Personal) | [LibreOffice](https://www.libreoffice.org/) | Documents, spreadsheets and slides | Desktop apps only: no 1 TB of cloud storage and no Copilot |
| Grammarly | $30/month (Pro) | [Harper](https://github.com/Automattic/harper) | Grammar and spelling checks that run on your machine | English only, and no AI rewriting |
| 1Password | $4.99/month (Individual) | [Bitwarden](https://github.com/bitwarden/clients) | Password manager for browser, desktop and phone | The free plan runs on Bitwarden's cloud with unlimited passwords and devices. Hosting it yourself is optional |

**Total: $151.96 a month.**
