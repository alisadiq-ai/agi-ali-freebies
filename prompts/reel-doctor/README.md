# Reel Doctor: 5 prompts that make Claude diagnose your reels

Claude can't tell you why a reel flopped until it can see your numbers. Connect FavStash once and these five prompts work on your real Instagram, TikTok and YouTube data.

## Before you start (2 minutes, free)

1. Connect FavStash to Claude: the one-prompt setup is at **[favstash.app/start](https://favstash.app/start)**. FavStash is listed in Claude's connector directory, and the free plan includes the connector.
2. Connect your Instagram (and TikTok or YouTube if you post there) inside FavStash.
3. Optional: save your own best reels to your FavStash stash too, so Claude can read their transcripts, not just their captions.

Analytics history depends on your FavStash plan: the free plan reads the last 7 days; paid plans read 30 or 90 days. Instagram reports skip rate and watch time per reel; it doesn't report second-by-second retention, so Claude won't invent one.

## The five prompts

**1. Which reels kept people past three seconds?**
```
Use FavStash to pull my most recent Instagram reels with their 3-second skip rate, average watch time, shares and saves. Rank them from the lowest skip rate to the highest. Then tell me what my top 3 do in the first second that my bottom 3 don't. Quote the first line of each caption.
```

**2. Which hooks did my top three open with?**
```
For my 3 reels with the lowest skip rate, give me the opening line of each (from my FavStash saves if the reel is saved there, otherwise from the caption). Name the hook shape of each: a number, a story, a question, a contrast or a line spoken to someone. What do the three have in common?
```

**3. Which app did this same video do best on?**
```
Find videos I posted to more than one platform (match them by caption or by posting time). For each one, compare views and average watch time on Instagram, TikTok and YouTube, and tell me which app it worked best on and what that suggests about where I should post first.
```

**4. What did I save that I haven't used?**
```
Search my FavStash saves from the last 30 days. Group them by idea, and list the 10 I haven't made my own version of yet, with the hook of each and why I probably saved it.
```

**5. Write my next hook from my best one.**
```
Take my lowest-skip reel from the first prompt. Write 3 openings for my next reel about [YOUR TOPIC] in the same shape, each under 12 words, with a matching on-screen text line. Say which of my saved reels each one borrows from.
```

Run them in order once a week: 1 and 2 tell you what's working, 3 tells you where, 4 and 5 turn it into your next reel.
