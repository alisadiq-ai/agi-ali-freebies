# 8 free Claude skills and plugins that help you show up on Google

For a small business, a shop or a blog that nobody finds. Each one is free and open source. Most run in Claude Code, which needs a paid Claude plan.

**Good to know:** these help you find and fix problems. Nothing can guarantee a ranking.

| # | Skill or plugin | What it does for you | Get it | Good to know |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | **Claude SEO** | `/seo audit` checks your whole site and ranks every fix; `/seo local` covers your Google Business Profile, reviews and the map pack; `/seo schema` adds the data Google reads | [AgriciDaniel/claude-seo](https://github.com/AgriciDaniel/claude-seo) | MIT. No API keys needed. An audit takes 10–15 minutes |
| 2 | **Marketing Skills** (`seo-audit`, `schema`, `ai-seo`, `content-strategy`) | Ask why a page won't rank and get the fixes, plus a content plan | [coreyhaines31/marketingskills](https://github.com/coreyhaines31/marketingskills) | MIT |
| 3 | **Anthropic's Marketing plugin** (`/seo-audit`) | An SEO audit from Anthropic's own plugin collection | [anthropics/knowledge-work-plugins](https://github.com/anthropics/knowledge-work-plugins) (`marketing`) | Apache-2.0. Works without paid tools; its Ahrefs and Similarweb connectors are optional and paid |
| 4 | **Chrome DevTools MCP** (`lighthouse_audit`) | Claude runs Lighthouse on your page and reads you the SEO score and every failing check | [ChromeDevTools/chrome-devtools-mcp](https://github.com/ChromeDevTools/chrome-devtools-mcp) | Apache-2.0, from Google's Chrome DevTools team. Page speed needs a separate trace |
| 5 | **Local SEO Optimizer** | Your Google Business Profile, reviews, address consistency and "near me" pages | [OneWave-AI/claude-skills](https://github.com/OneWave-AI/claude-skills) (`local-seo-optimizer`) | MIT. Uses Claude's built-in tools only |
| 6 | **Claude Blog** | A researched blog post, scored before you see it | [AgriciDaniel/claude-blog](https://github.com/AgriciDaniel/claude-blog) | MIT. Its AI images and narration are optional and use a paid Gemini key |
| 7 | **Google Search Console MCP** | Ask "which searches am I on page two for?" and get the answer from your own Search Console | [AminForou/mcp-gsc](https://github.com/AminForou/mcp-gsc) | MIT. Uses your own free Search Console. Setup takes a while (a Google Cloud project and sign-in) |
| 8 | **SearchFit SEO** | 11 SEO skills: audits, keywords, schema and content briefs | [searchfit/searchfit-seo](https://github.com/searchfit/searchfit-seo) | MIT. A small project, also listed in Claude's plugin directory; it points to the paid SearchFit platform |
