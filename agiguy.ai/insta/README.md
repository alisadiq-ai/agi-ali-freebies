# 9 free Claude skills for Instagram

Ideas, scripts, edits and numbers, all inside Claude. Each one is free (open source, or a free plan where noted). Most run in Claude Code, which needs a paid Claude plan.

| # | Skill | What it does for you | Get it | Good to know |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | **Marketing Skills** (`social`) | Ask "what should I post this week?" and get hooks, short-video scripts and a posting calendar | [coreyhaines31/marketingskills](https://github.com/coreyhaines31/marketingskills) | MIT |
| 2 | **YouTube Transcript MCP** | Paste a viral video's link and Claude reads its exact script and hook | [jkawamoto/mcp-youtube-transcript](https://github.com/jkawamoto/mcp-youtube-transcript) | MIT. YouTube can rate-limit it |
| 3 | **last30days** | What people are actually saying about a topic this month on Reddit, YouTube and more | [mvanhorn/last30days-skill](https://github.com/mvanhorn/last30days-skill) | MIT. Reddit and YouTube work out of the box; Instagram and TikTok need a ScrapeCreators key (limited free credits) |
| 4 | **Apify MCP + Instagram Scraper** | Pull any public account's reels with views, likes and comments, then ask Claude what's working | [apify/apify-mcp-server](https://github.com/apify/apify-mcp-server) | Free plan: $5 of usage a month, no card |
| 5 | **video-use** | Drop in raw takes; get back a cut with the "umms" removed and subtitles burned in | [browser-use/video-use](https://github.com/browser-use/video-use) | MIT. Uses an ElevenLabs key (the free plan has monthly credits; commercial use needs a paid plan) |
| 6 | **HyperFrames** | Describe a video and get an MP4 with animated captions and motion graphics | [heygen-com/hyperframes](https://github.com/heygen-com/hyperframes) | Apache-2.0. `claude plugin install hyperframes@hyperframes` |
| 7 | **Kinocut** | "The best 45 seconds of this interview, as a captioned vertical short" | [KyaniteLabs/kinocut](https://github.com/KyaniteLabs/kinocut) | Apache-2.0. Runs on your machine; needs FFmpeg |
| 8 | **FavStash Creator Skills** | Hand Claude a script, get a finished reel back: captions, 99 film looks, a 3D sitcom or a cartoon of you | [alisadiq-ai/favstash-ai-video-editing-skills](https://github.com/alisadiq-ai/favstash-ai-video-editing-skills) | Apache-2.0. Setup takes a while (FFmpeg, HyperFrames) |
| 9 | **FavStash connector** | Save reels you like, ask Claude what's working, and post to Instagram, TikTok and YouTube from Claude | [favstash.app/start](https://www.favstash.app/start) | Free to start, no card |

More every week on Instagram [@agiguy.ai](https://www.instagram.com/agiguy.ai/).
