# 5 things to build inside Claude in one weekend

Build each one once and it gets more useful every week. Each is one prompt. Turn the first, second and fifth into scheduled tasks (in Claude Code or the Claude app, ask Claude to "make this a scheduled task" with the time you want).

## 1. A morning brief: every weekday at 8:00

```text
Every weekday at 8:00, pull my growth numbers from [your tools: analytics, sign-ups, sales]. Compare them with yesterday and the same day last week. Send me five lines: what changed overnight, the one number to watch and the one thing to do today. Save each brief to [a folder or a Notion page] so you can compare weeks.
```

## 2. A creator radar: every Monday

```text
Every Monday, check the latest reels of the creators in [creators.txt]. Flag any reel doing at least 3x that creator's usual views. For each one, give me the hook word for word, the format and one line on why it worked. Save the list with the date.
```

Needs a connector that can read creators' reels, such as [Scrape Creators](https://scrapecreators.com/) or [FavStash](https://www.favstash.app/start).

## 3. A second brain

Save every reel you like to [FavStash](https://www.favstash.app/start). It lands with its transcript and its hook, and once FavStash is connected, Claude can search all of it:

```text
Search my saved reels for hooks about [topic]. Group them by pattern and tell me which pattern shows up most.
```

## 4. A script writer

```text
Here's a reel that went viral: [link or transcript]. Keep what made it work, the structure, the pacing and the turn, and write my version about [my topic] in my voice. Give me three different hooks. My voice: [paste a few of your own captions or a script you wrote].
```

## 5. The nudge: every 6 hours until you reply

```text
Every Sunday evening, review my week: what I shipped, what I said I'd do and didn't. Send it to me on Slack. Then message me every 6 hours until I reply. Stop as soon as I answer.
```

Needs the Slack connector.
