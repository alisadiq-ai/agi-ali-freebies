# 7 free Claude tools that fix your job applications

You still press submit. None of these applies for you; they fix what you send.

| # | Tool | What it does for you | Get it | Good to know |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | **career-ops** | Paste a job link: get a 1–5 fit score, a tailored CV, a cover letter, interview practice and a tracker | [career-ops-hq/career-ops](https://github.com/career-ops-hq/career-ops) | MIT. It never submits for you. Setup takes more than 5 minutes |
| 2 | **Resume Skills** | ATS check, tailoring, cover letters, salary negotiation and offer comparison | [Paramchoudhary/ResumeSkills](https://github.com/Paramchoudhary/ResumeSkills) | MIT |
| 3 | **Resume Tailoring Skill** | Builds a one-job resume from your old ones, and asks about experience you forgot to list | [varunr89/resume-tailoring-skill](https://github.com/varunr89/resume-tailoring-skill) | MIT. Needs Anthropic's document-skills for Word or PDF output |
| 4 | **PM Job Search** | Decodes what a job ad really wants, briefs you on the company, drafts outreach, preps the interview | [mohitagw15856/pm-claude-skills](https://github.com/mohitagw15856/pm-claude-skills) (`plugins/pm-jobsearch`) | MIT. Written for product managers; the job-ad decoder works on any ad |
| 5 | **Resume Panel** | Seven reviewers try to reject your resume, each on their own, then Claude rewrites it without inventing anything | [resume-panel](https://github.com/alisadiq-ai/agi-ali-freebies/tree/main/skills/resume-panel) | Apache-2.0. Works in the Claude app; best in Claude Code |
| 6 | **Resume Tailor** | A keyword match score against the job ad, plus rewritten bullets | [borghei/Claude-Skills](https://github.com/borghei/Claude-Skills) (`personal-productivity/resume-tailor`) | Free to use (MIT + Commons Clause) |
| 7 | **Job Search OS** | Runs your search inside the Claude app; drafts land in your own Gmail and Drive | [alexclowe/job-search-os](https://github.com/alexclowe/job-search-os) | MIT. A small new project, and it needs a paid Claude plan |

Several of these run in Claude Code, which needs a paid Claude plan.
