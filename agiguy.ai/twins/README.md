# 7 paid AI tools with a free twin

Seven AI subscriptions, each with a free, open-source app that does the job on your own computer. Together that's about **$89 a month**, at the lowest price each paid tool lists on its own pricing page (USD, checked November 20, 2026; most of those are annual plans, so month-to-month costs more).

**Before you start:** these run on your computer, so what you get depends on your machine. Vane, Dyad and Cline also need your own AI key or a local model, and that AI can cost money.

| # | Instead of | Use | What you get | Get it | Good to know |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | Suno ($8/mo) | **ACE-Step 1.5** | Type a description and get a full song with vocals, up to 10 minutes | [ace-step/ACE-Step-1.5](https://github.com/ace-step/ACE-Step-1.5) | MIT. Ready-made packages for Mac and Windows; runs on 6 GB of graphics memory or less (at lower quality) and on Apple Silicon. Its makers rate it between Suno v4.5 and v5 |
| 2 | ElevenLabs ($5/mo) | **VoxCPM2** | Clone a voice from a short clip and read any script, in 30 languages | [OpenBMB/VoxCPM](https://github.com/OpenBMB/VoxCPM) | Apache-2.0, free for commercial use. Needs about 8 GB of graphics memory or an Apple Silicon Mac. Installs with Python (`pip install voxcpm`), not one click |
| 3 | Adobe Firefly ($9.99/mo) | **Invoke** | Make and edit images on a real canvas: inpaint, outpaint, layers | [invoke.ai](https://invoke.ai) · [invoke-ai/InvokeAI](https://github.com/invoke-ai/InvokeAI) | Apache-2.0. Download-and-click launcher. Results depend on the free model you download. Apple Silicon Macs work (16 GB+ memory recommended) |
| 4 | Descript ($16/mo) | **Audapolis** | Edit a podcast or interview by deleting words from its transcript | [bugbakery/audapolis](https://github.com/bugbakery/audapolis) | AGPL-3.0. A small project. Covers Descript's edit-by-text trick, not its AI voices or green screen. Transcription runs on your computer |
| 5 | Perplexity Pro ($17/mo) | **Vane** (formerly Perplexica) | A private Perplexity-style answer engine with cited sources | [ItzCrazyKns/Vane](https://github.com/ItzCrazyKns/Vane) | MIT. Needs Docker, plus a local model or an AI API key. Not a five-minute setup |
| 6 | Bolt ($25/mo) | **Dyad** | Describe an app and watch it get built; the app and its code stay on your computer | [dyad.sh](https://dyad.sh) · [dyad-sh/dyad](https://github.com/dyad-sh/dyad) | Apache-2.0 (except its `src/pro` folder). Mac or Windows, no sign-up. Bring your own AI key or a local model |
| 7 | Cursor ($8/mo) | **Cline** | An AI coding agent inside the free VS Code editor that writes and edits code for you | [cline/cline](https://github.com/cline/cline) | Apache-2.0. The tool is free; the AI behind it costs money unless you run a local model (Ollama or LM Studio), which needs a strong computer |

Prices: Suno Pro $8/mo billed yearly ($10 month-to-month); ElevenLabs Starter $5/mo on an annual plan ($6 month-to-month); Adobe Firefly Standard $9.99/mo; Descript Hobbyist $16/mo billed yearly ($24 month-to-month); Perplexity Pro $17/mo billed yearly; Bolt Pro $25/mo billed monthly; Cursor Plus $8/mo, which includes Grok models only (Premium, with other models, is $30/mo). Counting Cursor at $30, the total is about $111 a month.
