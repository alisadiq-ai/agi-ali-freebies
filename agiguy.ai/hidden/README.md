# 9 Claude features most people never use, and where to click

All of these are in the Claude app already. Most work on every plan, including Free; the exceptions are marked.

| # | Feature | What it does | Where to click | Plans |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | **Skills** | Teach Claude a job once (your report format, your brand voice) and it uses it whenever it fits | Customize > Skills > **+** > Create skill, or Upload a skill. Settings > Capabilities > "Create and edit files" must be on | All plans |
| 2 | **Incognito chat** | A chat that stays out of your history and Claude's memory, and isn't used for training | The ghost icon, top right of a new chat (outside a project) | All plans. Still kept for 30 days by default, so it isn't deleted on the spot |
| 3 | **Quick entry** | Double-tap Option and Claude pops up over any app | Claude desktop app > Settings > Desktop app | Mac only |
| 4 | **Instructions for Claude** | Tell Claude once how you like answers, and it applies to every chat | Your initials > Settings > Your Claude | The help page doesn't list plans |
| 5 | **Effort and thinking** | Turn effort up and Claude thinks harder before it answers | Click the model name next to Send > Effort, and the Thinking toggle | The help page doesn't list plans. Some newer models think by default, so the toggle may not show |
| 6 | **Connectors** | Let Claude use your other apps inside a chat | **+** > Connectors in a chat; Customize > Connectors to add more | All plans. Free can add one custom connector |
| 7 | **Voice mode** | Talk to Claude out loud and hear the answer | The sound-wave icon | All plans. Beta |
| 8 | **Research** | Claude runs the searches and writes you a cited report | **+** > Research | Paid plans only |
| 9 | **Break reminders and quiet hours** | Reminders to take a break, plus quiet hours | Settings > Wellbeing | Free, Pro, Max and Ultra. Beta |

Menus move: if a click path has changed, search the Claude help centre for the feature's name.
