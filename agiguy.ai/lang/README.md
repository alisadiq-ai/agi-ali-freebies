# Language codes: 10 codes that turn Claude into your language tutor

Type a code, then your language and topic, for example `roleplay: ordering coffee in Spanish`. It's one free Claude skill: [language-codes](../../skills/language-codes/SKILL.md). It works for any language; Spanish is just the example in the reel.

The first time, Claude asks which language you're learning, your own language and your level, then keeps every answer at that level.

| # | Code | What Claude does |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | Roleplay | Plays the other person (a barista, a doctor, a landlord), one short line at a time, only in your new language. At the end: your three most useful corrections |
| 2 | Correct me | Fixes your sentence and explains the one mistake that matters most |
| 3 | Sound native | Shows how people actually say it, next to the textbook version, and whether it's casual or formal |
| 4 | Story mode | Writes a short story at your level from the words you're learning, then asks you three questions about it |
| 5 | One rule | One grammar rule in plain words, three examples, then three quick drills |
| 6 | Word hook | A silly sound-alike picture for each new word, then a quiz |
| 7 | Decode | Breaks down a menu, sign or message line by line, slang included |
| 8 | Translate back | Gives you sentences to translate, checks each one, and makes the next a little harder |
| 9 | Immersion | Answers only in your new language, at your level. Type "help" for an explanation |
| 10 | Daily ten | A ten-minute lesson: review, five new words, and a short roleplay that uses them |

**Good to know:** in a text chat Claude can't hear you, so for pronunciation it gives a sound-alike spelling and marks the stressed syllable. It says so when a phrase is regional or it isn't sure.

## Install

- **Claude app:** download the [language-codes](../../skills/language-codes) folder, zip it, and upload the zip in Customize > Skills (works on every plan).
- **Claude Code:** `npx skills add alisadiq-ai/agi-ali-freebies`, or copy the folder into `~/.claude/skills/`.
